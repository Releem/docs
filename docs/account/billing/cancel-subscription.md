# Cancellation

Before you begin, make sure you can sign in to your Releem account; after cancellation, you should receive a confirmation email from Paddle to keep for your records.

Follow these five steps to cancel your Releem subscription:

1. **Sign in.** Visit [Releem Login](https://app.releem.com) and enter your credentials to access your account.

2. **Open your profile.** Once you are signed in, select your account icon or name, usually located at the top right corner of the Dashboard, to open the Profile page.

3. **Find the cancellation link.** On the Profile page, locate the **Cancel Subscription** link. This link is integrated with Paddle, Releem's subscription management partner, to handle cancellations.

4. **Confirm the cancellation.** Select the **Cancel Subscription** link and follow the prompts to confirm your cancellation. You may be asked to provide feedback or a reason for cancellation.

5. **Keep the confirmation.** After completing the cancellation process, you should receive a confirmation email from Paddle. Keep this email for your records.

If you encounter any issues during the cancellation process, please contact Releem support for assistance.
